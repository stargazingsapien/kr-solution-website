# KR Solution — product and service storefront

Adapted from nexu-io/open-design's Refined design system (Apache-2.0).
Source: https://github.com/nexu-io/open-design/tree/main/design-systems/refined

Retain the source's minimal composition, explicit hierarchy, consistent 4/8/12/16/24/32 spacing, white card surfaces, restrained motion, strong focus indicators, and product-specific copy. Customize its palette and typography for KR Solution: pine #173f35, mint #d8efad, charcoal #182320, warm white #f7f8f5; Manrope and a restrained editorial serif accent. This is an adaptation, not a Shopify theme or the Open Design application.

Shop-first navigation with a separately discoverable Services route. Real photographs; no icon substitutes for product photos. Four product columns on desktop, two on mobile. Dark photographic hero with explicitly white text. 1280px content width, minimum 44px touch targets, 12px card corners. No purchase or payment actions.

The initial inventory is illustrative. The catalogue is now persisted on the local server and managed through the separate authenticated /admin interface. Stock 0, inactive, discontinued, or removed means hidden from the public catalogue API, category counts, filters, cards and product details. Service availability is independent. Enquiries do not change stock. No public admin buttons or stock-demo controls are shown.
